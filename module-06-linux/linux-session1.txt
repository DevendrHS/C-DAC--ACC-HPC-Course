Part 1----Introduction to UNIX---

unix origin---

.Developed at Bell Labs beginning in 1969

• Early contributors included Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie

• Reimplemented largely in C, improving portability across hardware

• Combined a small kernel with composable user-space tools

• Spread through universities and commercial vendors, creating multiple branches

unix philosophy--

• Build programs that do one focused job well

• Make program output suitable as another program’s input

• Prefer text streams and simple interfaces

• Compose solutions from small tools instead of one oversized utility

• Automate repeatable work through scripts
