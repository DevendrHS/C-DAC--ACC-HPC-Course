                  STP----steps
1. it send BPDU Packet send to every one
  it contain BridgeID of Switch
  BridgeID--VLAN + PRIORITY number + MAC address
        
        bydefault--VLAN1 and priority number is same for all 
        but mac adddress is always different to each other


  lower mac address will be declaired as root Bridge
  step 2-- designated port  is elected by cost of path 
where cost is less that is declared as  designated port

 step 3 . the port which is not elected will be declared as blocked port          


 5 state of STP  ---

 1. blocked---it detect broadcast storm
 2. listening--BpDU packet are sent
 3. learning--learning the packet
 4. forwarding--forwarding the packet
 5.Disable  --- work is done and Disable
